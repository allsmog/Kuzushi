# Study reader orientation

Fixed-study readers now lead with `Study jobs` or `Study job`, the selected
target, and reality-derived investigation progress. The secondary line labels
the current selection as `configured model`; a historical job retains its own
recorded model field. Global command routing no longer supplies the reader's
heading.

This is a presentation change within the existing context-strip view model.
It does not change input ownership, navigation, job membership, approval,
accounting, runtime selection or historical records. Active planning forms,
legacy history and other views keep their existing orientation. A parked draft
does not hide the currently open study reader. Short readers continue to
prioritize their body and controls when space cannot hold the full strip.
