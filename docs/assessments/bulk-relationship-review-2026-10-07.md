# Bounded bulk relationship review

Operators can review an explicit selection of up to fifty relationships and
record one decision atomically. Pair review, a required reason and final
confirmation precede execution. Finding identities, observations and verdicts
remain independent.

Pair review shows the available source claim and opens either endpoint's exact
recorded finding generation. `G` restores the selected pair, source side, scroll
and shell draft. Missing claims in the loaded page are disclosed and can be read
through the existing generation lookup rather than rebound to another finding.

The existing writer lock checks the complete captured relationship objects and
revisions. Acceptance checks current observation endpoints too. A stale record
or changed observation refuses the entire acceptance batch before any event.
Historical rejection and unlinking preserve the recorded endpoints. One durable
audit envelope makes successful batch replay indivisible; individual review
and undo remain supported through the shared reader.

The new diagnostic namespace requires upgrading all readers and writers before
using batch decisions. Existing single events need no migration. Older builds
ignore the new namespace and cannot accurately continue relationship review.
