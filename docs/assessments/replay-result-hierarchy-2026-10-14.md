# Saved results need meaning before long paths

The CodeQL audit's 40x12 captures showed a partial outcome followed by several
wrapped source-path rows. Two page-down presses still did not reach its detailed
limitation. A warning recorded in the authenticated result was not promoted
because Replay only promoted errors. The compact notice reservation was already
working; dismissing notices did not repair the result hierarchy.

Replay now leads with terminal status and the canonical recorded-cost label.
Partial runs with no recorded error or unavailable-evidence issue promote a
nonempty recorded warning. The warning is labeled and attributed to its reporting
tool; it is not inferred to explain an aggregate failure or establish a verdict.
Recorded errors and hydration failures take priority. Successful, active, blocked,
canceled and failed runs do not promote an unrelated historical warning.

The full terminal summary, source paths, reporting tool, cost, continuation,
result detail, disclosures and timeline remain accessible in the same document.
Their typed presentation and semantic identities are unchanged. A failure without
a recorded issue still shows its terminal explanation before other details.
Plain/no-color output uses the same reading surface. No receipt is rewritten,
notice dismissed, focus moved, model switched or investigation work created by
the presentation change.
