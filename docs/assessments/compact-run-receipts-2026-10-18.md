# Compact completion receipts retain complete results

Final completion notices previously copied a flattened result body after the
complete curated output had already entered the transcript. Long metadata
reports repeated source revisions, qualifications and diagnostics in the final
receipt and banner. At narrow widths that repetition pushed the outcome out of
the useful viewport.

Short results still lead with their actual text. Long or multiline results now
produce a compact receipt naming the tool, aggregate outcome, run count and
elapsed time, followed by `full result: logs`. The complete curated output stays
in its original transcript entry and canonical result artifact. Receipt result
projections and artifact identities are unchanged. This is a presentation
change, without a new command, module option or persisted format.

Compound or mismatched child results retain the overall outcome first. Current
errors, warnings and persistence failures keep their existing priority. The
completion publisher does not change the draft or navigation origin. Brief tool
timing events remain separate from final receipts; this increment does not
claim that every repeated diagnostic has been removed.
