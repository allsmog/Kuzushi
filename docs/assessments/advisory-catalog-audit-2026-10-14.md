# Finite advisory drafting retains spending and source receipts

`threat-intel:harvest` previously accepted an unlimited explicit ID list, dropped
nested extraction receipts and returned a completed parent even when every
entry failed. Both harvest and research were Core external actions without an
investigation allowance. After extraction's accounting fix, their real child
dispatch lacked the required inherited budget. Explicit parent model overrides
were also not forwarded to extraction.

Both actions are now Labs and auxiliary Map with Learning accounting. Existing
external authorization remains. Nested requests inherit the same approved
investigation work and selected model. Extraction options are shared from their
canonical Rust descriptors; reasoning and requested response/turn limits reach
the child without a per-mode path. No source tools are added by extraction.
Approval of these compound actions includes their declared finite drafting work;
they start no discovery hunts or additional worker jobs.

Harvest accepts at most 32 supplied rows, with one to 32 unique IDs and a default
of eight. IDs are nonblank, bounded to 128 bytes and limited to safe advisory
identifier characters. Exact case aliases collapse before paid work; distinct
IDs are retained. Research retains its package/query bounds and now passes at
most eight advisory IDs by default, maximum 32, to the same harvest implementation.
Its model-backed action may perform one initial extraction and one repair per
retained ID, subject to the shared allowance. Dollar consumption remains uncertain.

Supplied advisory, diff and OSV fixtures share the declared encoded metadata
bound before copying. Research's osvFixtures is optional; offline mode without
responses yields failed lookups. A supplied map never falls back to live
retrieval for absent packages. Package inventory remains partial and does not
prove advisory applicability. Live OSV and reference retrieval are unqualified.

The loop checks cancellation and available unprotected capacity before preparing
another advisory. This is a preflight check; reservation remains atomic at the
provider boundary. A concurrent worker can still consume capacity before the
request, which is then refused. The remaining explicit IDs are deferred after
the next capacity check. Completed draft rows and their child receipts remain
in the partial result and draft catalog. Missing/malformed child output records
failure rather than a completed clean result. Final publication preserves
already completed drafts after cancellation and never accepts their claims.

Parent results are partial with draft=true, accepted=false, patternsVerified=false
and coverage.complete=false for harvest. Research discloses incomplete inventory,
sourceVerified=false and accepted=false. Harvest rows retain extractionReceipt,
including the source-content digest, omissions, child status and consumption.
Catalog provenance marks the pattern unaccepted and source claims unverified.
Existing schema, entry identifiers and structural regression metadata remain.
Such a gate compares pattern hit counts; it is not a finding verdict. Legacy
severity/confidence defaults, append duplicates, full context retention and
whole-operation recovery remain unqualified.
