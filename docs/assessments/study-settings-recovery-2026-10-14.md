passes, workers, engine options, selected source/context, allowances to review,
usage baseline, scopes and accepted brief reference. Untouched forms and ordinary
navigation create no private record. Recovery distinguishes whole-repository

An optional version-1 `briefDraft` retains unaccepted multiline goal text, Unicode
cursor, reading position, prior immutable revision and editor mode. Earlier
records without the field remain readable. Recovering an interrupted save returns
to review and clears pending-save identity; it does not replay publication.
Reloads retain raw text. A changed accepted head refuses a stale save. Explicit
reload, review and save produce a new revision. A different unsaved session goal
blocks restoration rather than being overwritten.

Acknowledged atomic replacement resolves only adopted hashes while retaining
independent later edits. Clearing context cases preserves other study settings.
Successful finite-plan preparation resolves the draft when no unsaved goal
remains. Recovery restores no plan authorization, provider runtime or spending
approval; existing accounting is still read from its original investigation.

Pending or failed ordinary writes retain the form on Back. A visible Stay or
Discard choice protects settings and unsaved goal text. Discard does not delete
accepted briefs, plans, observations, decisions or accounting. Pending or failed
resolution remains visible. Quit names uncheckpointed goals without exposing
their text. Validation/enqueue failures show their cause instead of borrowing
an older save acknowledgment. The brief editor keeps failure messages before
local-save status and adjusts paging to the actual available body rows.

## Scope and remaining work

This qualifies discovery-study forms, including scope and context comparisons.
Context-generation and Review forms keep their distinct orchestration and are
not reinterpreted as discovery on recovery. Uncheckpointed goal text on those
surfaces is disclosed. Extending their full draft recovery, large-history input
latency, kernel IO stalls, cross-platform race behavior, fresh live scope utility
and complete reference-quality visual acceptance remain open. Pending writes
can still be lost in a crash. No complete-plan or full Rust-suite claim is made.
