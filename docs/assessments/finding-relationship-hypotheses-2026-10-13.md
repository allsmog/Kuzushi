# Bounded finding-relationship hypotheses

`triage:chain-hunt` produces new unreviewed observations from confirmed finding
metadata. It does not record verdicts for those members or establish that their
interaction is real. The action is now Labs and **Propose finding relationships**,
retaining its exact name, Review authorization and existing result fields.
It has no Review checkpoint and uses Discovery accounting so optional synthesis
cannot consume the protected Review allocation.

The former validator silently removed unknown members. A proposal containing two
valid fingerprints plus an invented third could survive with an explanation
that no longer matched its members. Every member must now resolve exactly,
with two to 32 distinct references. Duplicate, unknown or nontext members reject
the whole proposal. More than 128 proposals fails the structured contract.
Rejected or omitted proposals produce a partial receipt, preserving valid
hypotheses and their limitations.

Declared limits bound model inputs and outputs: maxChains is one to 32,
maxCandidates two to 200, maxPriorChains zero to 64, maxContextBytes 1,024 to
524,288 bytes and maxOutputTokens 256 to 16,384 per request. Defaults are eight,
100, 32, 65,536 and 4,096 respectively. The registered integer ranges enforce
those values through shared preparation; compatibility snake-case aliases remain.
Metadata selection is deterministic. Titles, paths and messages are abbreviated
with their limits disclosed; fingerprints over 256 bytes are refused. An
oversized metadata prompt fails before a request. inputCoverage reports supplied
and eligible candidates and prior hints; omissions are partial, not complete
coverage.

The prompt-byte cap covers assembled metadata, excluding system/schema and
runtime additions. Optional narrative fields retain their historical blank
defaults; malformed or oversized optional values can therefore become empty.
This audit does not claim validation of every output field or a byte bound on
the complete transport request.

The request uses a nondefault empty tool policy and one model turn, inheriting
selected reasoning. It supplies no source tools and never starts another job.
The existing single structured repair remains separately budgeted. The legacy
attack_path field now carries high-level source-analysis questions; instructions
exclude exploit, payload, reproducer and patch development. No generated code or
source execution is involved in this audit.

Confirmed composite findings cannot become new base inputs. Prior hypotheses
remain bounded hints, not permission for recursive work. Cancellation is checked
before work and after the response. Before publication, every member must still
be confirmed with the same finding generation and observation run. A concurrent
FP decision or changed observation prevents publication. The remaining race
between that check and persistence is not transactionally closed.

Existing chains, confirmedCount, droppedProposals and persistedRunId fields stay
available. Additive hypotheses, relationshipsVerified, sourceRevisionVerified
and inputCoverage fields identify what the result establishes. New findings are
explicitly unreviewed metadata hypotheses and preserve member decisions. Valid
metadata synthesis may complete its own receipt, while the descriptor prevents
Review completion. An empty valid result never resolves any member.
